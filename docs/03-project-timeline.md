03 — Project Timeline

Historical dates for individual operations are not recorded. The project period is given by the author as April–September 2026.

Stage 1 — MIUI 12.5

The Redmi Note 8 began with MIUI 12.5.

Stage 2 — Preparation and bootloader unlock

The phone was factory-reset and connected to a Xiaomi account. The author used Xiaomi's Mi Unlock website/tool and unlocked the bootloader.

Stage 3 — OrangeFox R10.0

OrangeFox R10.0 was installed using ADB/Fastboot commands.

Stage 4 — Pixel 3.6 experiment

The author used OrangeFox to perform the Pixel installation process, including flashing a Pixel recovery image and installing the Pixel 3.6 ZIP.

The exact Pixel build/version and filenames were not retained.

Stage 5 — Bootloop

The phone became stuck in a repeated boot cycle:

Boot → power off/restart → boot → repeat

The author was relatively new to Android modification at the time and spent approximately four days troubleshooting before the phone successfully booted into Pixel 3.6.

Stage 6 — Pixel testing

Major device functions were tested. The system was usable, but rapid battery drain was observed.

The project record does not establish the battery-drain cause experimentally.

Stage 7 — Termux/Linux

The author began learning basic Linux/Ubuntu-style commands through Termux. The first attempt was unsuccessful; a later attempt was successful.

Stage 8 — Recovery loop

The device later repeatedly entered recovery instead of normal Android after power cycling.

The author entered Fastboot and installed a newer OrangeFox recovery, R12.0.

Stage 9 — LineageOS 23.2 / Android 16

The author decided to move to LineageOS 23.2 / Android 16.

Current evidence confirms the resulting installation.

Stage 10 — Functional testing

The current system was tested for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, camera, flashlight, speaker, microphone, USB, charging, touchscreen, display, sensors, battery, performance, storage, Termux/Linux commands, ADB, and Fastboot.

Evidence limitation

No historical screenshots or personal notes are available for the early stages. The historical timeline is therefore explicitly a reconstructed first-person account supported where possible by the original project report
