# 06 — Pixel 3.6 Experiment

## Objective

The author wanted to move away from the original MIUI 12.5 environment and experiment with a custom Android system.

## Recorded process

The author reported:

1. Opening OrangeFox.
2. Flashing the relevant old OS/system components.
3. Flashing a Pixel recovery `.iso`.
4. Opening the Pixel recovery.
5. Selecting the Pixel 3.6 `.zip`.
6. Installing the OS.

## Result

The phone initially entered a bootloop.

After approximately four days of troubleshooting, the device successfully booted into Pixel 3.6.

## Limitations

The exact Pixel build, recovery filename, firmware version, and hashes are not available in the surviving records.

## Battery observation

After successful boot, rapid battery drain was observed.

The project does not establish whether the drain was caused by the ROM, software configuration, or the device's aged battery/hardware history.

## Historical evidence

No screenshots from the Pixel 3.6 period are available.
