# 13 — Lessons Learned

## Technical lessons

- Bootloader unlocking is a prerequisite for many Android system modifications.
- ADB and Fastboot are important Android system-management tools.
- Custom recovery provides capabilities beyond stock recovery.
- ROM, recovery, firmware, and device compatibility matter.
- A failed flash does not necessarily mean permanent device failure.
- Recovery and Fastboot access are critical during troubleshooting.
- Linux fundamentals are useful for deeper Android and cybersecurity learning.

## Problem-solving lessons

The project demonstrated the importance of:

- patience during boot failures
- checking system state before taking another action
- understanding recovery/bootloader modes
- avoiding assumptions about failure causes
- documenting successful and unsuccessful experiments

## Documentation lessons

The project began without screenshots or personal research notes. As a result, early historical events can be reconstructed but cannot all be independently demonstrated through contemporaneous evidence.

Future experiments will be recorded immediately with dates, screenshots, commands, results, and observations.
