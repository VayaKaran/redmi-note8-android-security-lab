# 05 — Recovery History

## OrangeFox R10.0

OrangeFox R10.0 was the first custom recovery recorded by the author.

It was used during the Pixel 3.6 experiment.

## OrangeFox R12.0

After the later recovery-loop problem, the author entered Fastboot and flashed a newer OrangeFox recovery, identified as R12.0.

The phone was subsequently moved to LineageOS 23.2 / Android 16.

## Important limitation

The exact recovery image filenames, hashes, and download dates were not retained.

They should not be reconstructed from memory.

## Recovery learning outcome

The project provided hands-on exposure to:

- recovery environments
- Fastboot
- ROM flashing
- boot failure
- recovery-loop troubleshooting
- reinstallation of recovery
